Money in football

Transfer market

When a player moves to a new club while under contract, the new club pays a transfer fee. Here is how the record grew from £3 million to over €200 million, the biggest deal of every year, and who is worth most today.

Transfer words explained
Transfer fee
Money the buying club pays the selling club to end the player's contract early.
Add-ons
Extra money paid later if targets are met, for example titles or appearances. That is why sources give different fees.
Release clause
A fixed price written in the contract. If a club pays it, the player can leave. Neymar's €222m was his release clause.
Free transfer
When a contract ends, the player can join any club for no fee (the "Bosman" rule, 1995).
Loan
A player plays for another club for a season, then returns.
Transfer window
The periods when clubs may register new players: summer (around June to 1 September) and January.
