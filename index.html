Five things to watch for

1Who has the ball?

The team in possession attacks; the other team tries to win it back. Watch how quickly the ball changes hands.

2The shape

Teams keep a formation, like 4-3-3. When they lose the ball, players run back to fill their spaces.

3The offside line

Defenders step up together to catch attackers offside. A goal can be ruled out for a few centimetres.

4Set pieces

Corners and free kicks are planned moves. Many goals come from them, so this is a good moment to pay attention.

5The referee

A raised arm means an indirect free kick. Arms pointing forward mean the referee is playing advantage.
